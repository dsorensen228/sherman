# Project Assets

`concept/` holds product-intent renders and the broad goal sheet. Future directories may hold display assets, diagrams and governed demo media. Generated assets must record source/prompt/tool/license where applicable and be referenced by an editable or reproducible source when practical.

Do not put raw private camera captures, credentials, vendor files without redistribution rights, or large untracked test output here. Test evidence belongs under `artifacts/` or an external manifest-controlled store.
