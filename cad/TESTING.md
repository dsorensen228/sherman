# CAD and Mechanical Testing Plan

## Automated/design checks

Rebuild/export from clean editable sources; no missing references; parameter bounds; nonmanifold/interference/thin-wall checks; mass/CoM estimate; envelope; wheel/ground clearance; component/connector/service volumes; FOV/baffle clearance; fastener/tool access; naming/version and derived-export freshness.

## Fit and inspection

Print progressive coupons/sections before full chassis for board mounts, motor press/fastener fit, wheel/shaft, inserts, display/window, FPC/harness and sensor apertures. Compare as-built dimensions against model; feed systematic printer/material shrink into profile, not arbitrary part edits.

## Assembly

Ten full service cycles using the written order. Record duration, required tools, cable/connector damage, lost fasteners, insert torque/retention, calibration shift and operator ambiguity. Battery and motor disable must be reachable without destructive or unsafe handling.

## Drivetrain

Wheel runout/alignment, track/axle geometry, chassis rocking/skid load, ground clearance, traction/desk marking, debris/hair ingestion, noise/vibration, motor temperature and fastener loosening. Repeat after endurance.

## Safety/optical/thermal

Static tilt/tip and CoM; cliff sensor height/angle/tolerance and approach coverage; controlled handling/drop only under reviewed protocol; camera/ToF FOV and crosstalk through final face; display reflection/light leak; antenna signal comparison; thermal steady state/deformation/battery-adjacent temperature.

## Release evidence

Measured final envelope/mass/CoM, component and drawing revision, material/print profile, dimensional inspection, photos, assembly report, test results, known tolerances and approved deviations. Artistic renders never count as mechanical evidence.
