# Product and Experience Specification

## Core promise

Sherman is a small embodied coworker who maintains a modest but persistent understanding of the owner’s desktop. The experience should read as “earnest tiny custodian/explorer,” not surveillance camera, remote-controlled toy, or omniscient assistant.

## Primary daily loop

1. Wake and self-test visibly.
2. Re-establish where he is and whether the qualified surface still looks safe.
3. Check a small set of routine desk landmarks.
4. Spend bounded free time resolving useful uncertainty or observing change.
5. Accept/cancel/defer Jarvis requests with honest progress.
6. Rest/charge and respect quiet/pause/privacy modes.
7. Summarize notable changes or report no material change.

## Legible behavior rules

- Look before moving toward or reporting a target.
- Expression and body orientation should agree with attention.
- Hesitation means uncertainty/validation, not random delay.
- Frustration changes presentation/retry utility, never safety limits.
- Success follows actual completion evidence.
- Failure becomes a bounded recovery or explicit help request.
- Pickup stops/disarms and produces an unmistakable placement state.
- Low battery, privacy, update, degraded and fault visuals are authoritative.

## Interaction modes

### Autonomous

Routine patrol and curiosity are duty/energy/risk bounded. Sherman explains the selected question via telemetry and can be paused immediately.

### Jarvis-directed

Jarvis requests an outcome—observe, locate, inspect, patrol, report—not raw motor duty. Sherman returns accepted/deferred/rejected, action progress and confidence-aware result. Cancellation wins unless a safety recovery must complete first.

### Human physical

The owner can pause/stop, pick up/place, charge/service, set quiet/privacy, clear/export state and ask Jarvis for status. Sherman should not require a phone UI for immediate motor disable.

## Personality boundaries

Sherman can be curious, pleased, tired-looking and mildly indignant as an interface metaphor. Do not claim consciousness, suffering, emotional dependence or secret knowledge. Do not use guilt to encourage engagement. Do not hide uncertainty/failure behind jokes.

## Environmental assumptions

V1 is for a reviewed indoor desktop with qualified surface reflectivity, edge geometry, lighting and clutter. It is not for floors, stairs, outdoors, wet/dirty areas, children/pets, delicate collections, loose hair/string hazards or unattended operation. Setup must record or communicate limitations.

## Experience acceptance

- Observers identify core intent and reserved status from short clips.
- Pause/stop/quiet/privacy are immediate and obvious.
- A full day has purposeful variety without constant motion/noise.
- Reports match evidence/confidence and avoid false certainty.
- Autonomous actions terminate, recover or ask for help.
- Character survives network loss but never interferes with safety.
