# Industrial Design Brief

## Intent

Sherman should look like a small credible instrument softened into a character: warm, quiet, serviceable and at home beside a keyboard. Character comes from the black eye window, rounded shell, low stance and motion—not decorative limbs or pet anatomy.

## Visual language

- Warm ivory/light neutral matte upper shell.
- Graphite/charcoal durable lower chassis and wheel region.
- Glossy or satin-black front face with a wide `320 × 170` display composition and high-contrast simple cyan-white eyes.
- Camera and ToF apertures are small, aligned and visually subordinate.
- Fine service seam, restrained vents and one subtle emergency/service accent if required.
- No mouth, arms, ears, antenna decoration, tank treads, exposed boards or logos in v1 concept.

Concept references are `assets/concept/sherman-final-product-render.png` and `sherman-desk-context.png`. They establish mood and proportions only.

## Physical priorities

1. Edge safety and stable support polygon.
2. Motor/battery electrical and service safety.
3. Optical performance and cleanable windows.
4. Recovery USB/controls and disassembly.
5. Thermal/antenna performance.
6. Display legibility and gaze alignment.
7. Target envelope and mass.
8. Cosmetic refinement.

## Ergonomics/service

Owner can identify front/state, stop motor power, connect recovery, clean face/wheels, inspect battery, and open shell with ordinary tools. No loose panel gives access to a live pinch/short hazard. Fasteners are retained/counted; repeated assembly does not move calibrated sensors beyond accepted residuals.

## Scale and proportion

Stretch `70 × 60 × 45 mm`, comfortable `≤75 × 65 × 50 mm`, mass `≤180 g`; measured engineering may enlarge them. Wheels appear integrated, not monster-truck-like. Face active area should read from seated distance without making sensor openings resemble a security camera.

The primary display carrier is `51.2 × 27.3 mm` with a `42.72 × 22.70 mm` active area. The face cassette must center the active area, disguise the unequal PCB/bezel margins, route the MX1.25 cable without an impossible bend, preserve fastener/window tolerances, and leave distinct baffled camera/ToF apertures. Do not scale concept art around a 4-inch or square panel.

## Finish validation

Evaluate reflectivity/light leak/IR transmission, fingerprints/cleaning, desk marking, print/manufacturing artifacts, acoustic resonance, vent/debris, color/brightness in daylight/night and thermal aging. Cosmetic material follows safe prototype validation.
